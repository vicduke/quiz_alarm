BrainWake mini emulator

The real app code, running in the browser with a simulated alarm engine. Data is kept in this browser.

  • Add an alarm, then tap its bell icon to make it ring in 3 seconds.
  • "Restart app" while it rings or is snoozed checks that the alarm survives.
  • Question Banks and Free Revision use the real SQLite database (in the browser).
  • AI features need your Gemini key in Settings, same as on the phone.
  • Not simulated: lock screen, notifications, vibration, battery limits. Those still need a real phone.
